Single-cell foundation models. Geneformer (Theodoris et al. , 2023 ) introduced a rank-encoded BERT-style transformer trained with masked-language-modeling on ≈ 30 \approx 30 M cells. scGPT (Cui et al. , 2024 ) extended this to generative pretraining over 33 33 M CELLxGENE Census cells with binned expression values. UCE (Rosen et al. , 2023 ) departs from gene-token approaches entirely, using ESM2 protein embeddings (Lin et al. , 2023 ) as the input tokens. scFoundation (Hao et al. , 2024 ) and several other models (Boiarsky et al. , 2023 ) have followed in rapid succession. All draw heavily on the same public scRNA-seq repositories. 3 Methodology scContam labels every cell c c in a benchmark ℬ \mathcal{B} with a contamination score against a reference pretraining corpus ℛ \mathcal{R} . Two complementary signals operate at different scales: Signal A (gene-set fingerprint) detects distributional contamination; MIA-scFM detects instance-level memorization. This dose-response has a clear methodological consequence. When applied to a production scFM trained with standard regularization on tens of millions of cells (capacity/cell ≪ 100 \ll 100 ), loss-based MIA is expected to return chance-level AUROC even when the model has seen the test cells. Future work characterizing this translation across multiple integration benchmarks would strengthen the practical recommendation. 6 Conclusion We have introduced scContam, a tractable per-cell audit framework for pretraining contamination in single-cell foundation model benchmarks. The framework combines a gene-set fingerprint signal (Signal A) against the explicit pretraining corpus, validated on 10 6 10^{6} Genecorpus-30M reference cells, with a loss-based membership inference attack (MIA-scFM) whose sensitivity is characterized via a controlled three-regime re-pretraining experiment. The audit identifies two widely-used benchmarks (PBMC 3k, pancreatic islet) as extensively in Geneformer’s training corpus and two post-cutoff benchmarks (AIDA v2, Tahoe-100M) as clean. A donor-matched within-cell-type analysis on pancreas shows that contaminated cells embed measurably more tightly than donor-matched clean cells across three foundation model architectures, with a permutation p ≤ 0.03 p\leq 0.03 in all three models and a perfectly null negative control. The single-cell foundation model field is now at a point where multiple architectures, multiple pretraining corpora, and multiple benchmarks are all in circulation. Per-cell pretraining audits should accompany zero-shot benchmark reporting.
