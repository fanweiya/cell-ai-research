STRATA introduces an RL-driven sampling agent on a 3D graph, leveraging single-cell prototype priors to capture cellular rarity and spatial redundancy. Building upon this, a tri-paradigm adaptation network further performs soft routing by assigning preference weights over complementary strategies. Extensive experiments demonstrate that STRATA consistently outperforms current methods. By recasting ST as a co-optimized sampling-prediction problem over 3D voxel volumes, STRATA charts a scalable and cost-effective path toward 3D tissue-level molecular mapping.
